# unit_converter_app
Made with FlutterFlow

Live App: https://unit-converter-tjpnsa.flutterflow.app

Features:
-Convert Cups, Grams, Tablespoons, Milliliers, Teaspoons

Examples:
- 1 Cup = 240 Milliliters (mL)
- 1 Cup = 16 Tablespoons
- 1 Cup = 48 Teaspoons
- 1 Tablespoon = 15 Milliliters (mL)
- 1 Tablespoon = 3 Teaspoons
- 1 Teaspoon = 5 Milliliters (mL)
- 1 Cup Water = 240 Grams
- 1 Cup Flour = 120 Grams
- 1 Cup Sugar = 200 Grams
